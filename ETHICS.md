# Ethics Statement

This repository is for *educational purposes only*.  
All scripts, writeups, and examples are created and tested in *legal lab environments* (e.g., CTFs, virtual labs, or platforms like TryHackMe/HTB).  

❌ Do NOT use any code, tools, or methods in this repo against systems without *explicit permission*.  
✅ Always follow local laws and responsible disclosure guidelines.  

The goal: to *learn, practice, and share knowledge* for building a career in ethical hacking — not for malicious activity.
